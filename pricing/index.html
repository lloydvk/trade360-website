Tailored pricing and support.

How your first month works
  • Every plan starts with 50% off your first month's subscription.
  • The one-off onboarding fee is charged alongside it at signup, so we can begin your setup straight away.
  • You get full access from day one — there's no waiting period.
  • From month two you're billed the full monthly price, on a rolling monthly contract you can cancel, upgrade or downgrade any time.
  • Enterprise is scoped and quoted individually — book a demo instead.
How AI credits work
  • Every AI-assisted action — drafting a quote, writing a follow-up, transcribing a call — uses a set amount of credits from your plan's monthly allowance.
  • Credits reset at the start of each billing cycle and don't roll over.
  • If you reach your limit, AI actions pause until your next cycle unless you add more credits as a top-up.
