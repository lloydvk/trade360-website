The name

This solution is actually born from businesses — not dreamed up by people who've never had to chase a late payment or fit in a quote between jobs.

Where AI fits

Selling is still a people business.

No system will ever replace the trust built face to face, the handshake that closes a job, or a tradesperson reading what a customer actually needs. That stays with you — it's the part of the business only a person can do.

What Trade 360 takes off your plate is everything around it: the chasing, the admin, the back-and-forth that eats the headspace you need for the decisions that actually move your business forward.

You build the relationship. The conversations, the trust, the key decisions — that's where you're needed most, and where you should be spending your time.
AI clears the way. Everything else — the follow-ups, the paperwork, the busywork — handled quietly in the background, so it's not taking up headspace it doesn't need to.

AI will never run your business.
But it will support you, and help you thrive.
